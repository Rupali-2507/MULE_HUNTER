01THE PROBLEM LANDSCAPE

UPI’s velocity is its greatest vulnerability.

India’s UPI network settles over ₹20 lakh crore per month across 13+ billion monthly transactions. That same settlement speed — engineered for inclusion — gives organised fraud rings a structural advantage over every rule-based detection system.

Individual Money Mules

  • Recruited via social engineering — students, migrant workers, low-income individuals
  • Accounts remain dormant (“sleeper” phase) until activated by a controller
  • Serve as disposable on-ramps for illicit funds entering the payment system
  • Typically transact within velocity thresholds, evading rule-engine flags

Collusive Fraud Networks

  • Coordinated rings of 10–200+ accounts operating in concert
  • Smurfing: large sums decomposed into sub-threshold micro-transfers
  • Layering: multi-hop chains that sever the origin ↔ destination link
  • Adversarially designed to evade every per-account, per-transaction rule

Why Existing Systems Fail

Rule engines operate on isolated transaction features. They have no concept of the relational graph that makes a pattern fraudulent. A smurfing network of 50 accounts looks like 50 normal users — unless you observe their collective topology.

The Scale of the Crisis

FATF estimates $3–5 trillion in annual global money laundering. RBI data indicates UPI fraud complaints grew over 300% from 2020–2024. Existing detection frameworks were not architected for graph-scale analysis.

The Missing Layer

No production system today fuses real-time graph topology scoring, behavioural anomaly detection, TLS device forensics, and tamper-proof audit into a single sub-500ms decision pipeline. That unified layer is MULE HUNTER.

06GRAPH NEURAL NETWORK INTELLIGENCE

From isolated events to structural patterns.

Star topology — Smurfing detection

Fifty sub-threshold transfers from a single hub to dispersed leaf accounts is undetectable per-transaction. GraphSAGE identifies the star pattern and flags the hub in real time via message-passing aggregation across all leaf neighbours.

Inductive inference — Zero retraining

GraphSAGE samples a new account’s neighbourhood to produce an embedding without retraining. An account seen for the first time is scored within one inference pass (<50ms p99) using its existing graph neighbours.

SHAP explainability — Regulatory compliance

Every blocked transaction is accompanied by machine-generated SHAP attributions: “High fan-in velocity,” “Circular flow signature,” “Shared device cluster.” Decisions are fully auditable and legally defensible.

Collective institutional memory

Confirmed fraud accounts are written to the immutable Merkle-tree ledger. Cross-institution lookups prevent the same actor from re-emerging at a different bank — the system learns collectively, not in isolation.

08KNOWN CONSTRAINTS & MITIGATIONS

We name our limits before you do.

Intellectual honesty is a component of engineering rigour. Every constraint below has a concrete mitigation pathway that does not require the core architecture to change.

Dataset scale gap

The current GNN was trained on a synthetic graph derived from the IEEE-CIS dataset — 14,318 nodes versus the tens of millions present in a production bank graph. GDPR and PCI-DSS constraints prevent access to real transaction data at this stage.

Mitigation Shadow-mode pilot with anonymised PSP data

Cold-start account problem

GraphSAGE requires a minimum of one graph neighbour to produce a structural embedding. Brand-new isolated accounts lacking any edges do not benefit from GNN-based scoring in their first transactions.

Mitigation JA3 + EIF hybrid scoring until graph degree ≥ 3

GPU infrastructure dependency

The GNN inference layer requires GPU acceleration at production-scale graph sizes. Estimated recurring cost of ~$3–5K/month per production cluster. Kubernetes orchestration is required for horizontal auto-scaling under UPI peak load.

Mitigation Spot GPU instances + O(1) logit cache

Regulatory integration pathway

Legacy bank rails operate on ISO 8583 protocols. Multi-institution federation requires PII anonymisation by design and formal RBI/NPCI approval before AI-driven transaction blocking can be activated in production.

Mitigation Advisory-mode deployment; alert-only first phase
